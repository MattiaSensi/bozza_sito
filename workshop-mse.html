Workshop MSE

The workshop brings together mathematicians who build reliable models for epidemiology and for social and population dynamics, and who use those models alongside experiments. It covers rigorous model building, dependable computing tools, and their use in control and decision-support strategies. The aim is to discuss methods, new techniques, research directions and interdisciplinary work.
