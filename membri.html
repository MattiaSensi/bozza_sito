Members

Membership requires being a UMI member; applications are made through the UMI website. The group aims to promote comparison and collaboration among researchers who use mathematical, statistical and computational methods to model socio-epidemiological questions.

    In memoriam

    The page remembers Maurizio Falcone, a founding member of the group who died in November 2022. He was a professor of numerical analysis at Sapienza University of Rome, a researcher with wide interests, and a colleague who encouraged people at every career stage. He spoke at the 2022 CIME school on mathematical modelling for epidemiology in Cetraro. The note is signed by the steering committee: Dimitri Breda, Fasma Diele, Andrea Pugliese, Cinzia Soresina and Mattia Zanella.
