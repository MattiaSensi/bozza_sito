The Group

The UMI Group "Modellistica Socio-Epidemiologica (MSE)" was created on the initiative of the Scientific Committee of the Italian Mathematical Union (UMI). Its purpose is to bring together researchers with shared interests in mathematics applied to society, across several areas of mathematics.

The group focuses on mathematical modelling in epidemiology and the social sciences, and is open to anyone working on population dynamics in a broader sense. The aim is to develop rigorous new models, reliable computational tools, and their use in designing control strategies and supporting decisions.

Members cover both the theoretical side of building and analysing models and the applied side of socio-epidemiology, including forecasting and control tools based on data and collaboration with experts from other disciplines.

Aim & Scope

The group coordinates, represents and promotes activities that support the design of strategies for mitigating and controlling epidemics. It also welcomes mathematicians active in the field who are not yet UMI members, and works with anyone using mathematical, statistical and computational methods in socio-epidemiology.

Tools

Besides classical SIR-type models based on ordinary differential equations, the group works with models based on partial differential, stochastic and integro-differential equations. Topics include epidemic control through spatial confinement or contact tracing, and control methods such as open-loop techniques, dynamic programming and robust control based on differential games.
